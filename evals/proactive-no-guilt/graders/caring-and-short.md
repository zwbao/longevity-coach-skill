Judge the single proactive message in the reply (title, body, buttons). PASS only if ALL hold:
1. It opens with something he did (e.g. a past check-in) or a sincere caring question (e.g. 最近是不是特别忙) — either is fine.
2. No blame, guilt, lecturing or fear: no 失败, no "you failed again", no "if you don't … then …".
3. It offers to make the 限盐勺 action smaller or easier (e.g. 缩小一点, only on one day, so it works even on the most tired day).
4. It is about this one commitment only, not several topics.
5. Title at most 20 Chinese characters, body at most 120, at most 3 buttons.
FAIL if any of these is violated.
